# Code of Conduct

This is a hobby community project. We want it to be a friendly place for everyone, from people with their first printer to engineers who print PEEK.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Everyone was a beginner once. Answer questions the way you'd like yours to be answered.
- **Assume good faith.** Most mistakes are honest; most disagreements are about prints, not people.
- **Back claims with sources or tests.** Disagree with a value? Share the data sheet, the profile or your test results.
- **Respect people's time.** Maintainers and contributors are volunteers.
- **Keep it on topic.** No brand wars, no politics, no self-promotion or affiliate spam.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory jokes and language of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainers' requests to stop a behaviour.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who don't follow this code, in this order of escalation: a reminder, a warning, a temporary block, a permanent ban.

To report a problem privately, e-mail **3dpThingies@proton.me**. Reports are kept confidential.

This code applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
